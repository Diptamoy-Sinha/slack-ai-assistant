# Henry Cavendish (1731–1810)

Credited, jointly with [[antoine-lavoisier]], with the discovery of the
composition of water:

> "The composition of water was discovered at the same period, both by Mr.
> Cavendish, in this country, and by the celebrated French chemist Lavoisier."
> `[COC-1809 p.91 (scan 117)]`

"In this country" is inherited verbatim from Marcet's London original — an
artefact of reprinting in New Haven without adjusting the text.

Also credited with establishing the nature and composition of **nitric acid**:
"Its Nature and Composition discovered by Mr. Cavendish"
`[COC-1809 contents (scan 10)]`.

Marcet gives Cavendish and Lavoisier equal billing and does not adjudicate
priority — a live dispute at the time, and one she declines to enter. What she
gives instead is Lavoisier's apparatus, in detail, because it is the one that
demonstrates the point quantitatively. See [[composition-of-water]].
