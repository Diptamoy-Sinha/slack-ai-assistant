# Recipes for the Manufacture of Aerated & Mineral Waters (1897, fifth edition)

**Full title:** *Recipes for the Manufacture of Aërated & Mineral Waters and
Non-Alcoholic Cordials*, compiled for [[w-j-bush-and-co]], Limited, by
[[baron-de-bush]], F.C.S., F.S.S. `[BUSH-1897 title page (scan 5)]`
**Printer:** H. Little & Son, Bishopsgate Street, London E.C.
**Date:** 1897. Fifth edition.
**Digitised:** 136 scan pages; one page has no text layer. Othmer Library of
Chemical History / Chemical Heritage Foundation bookplate at scan 5.

## What it is

A trade manual and a sales catalogue in one binding. Bush & Co. sold essences,
preservatives, and mineral-water salts; the recipes exist to be made with Bush
products, and roughly a third of the volume is advertising — for bottling
machines, glass and stoneware bottles, sugar, bicarbonate of soda, steam
engines, and Bush's own essence range. Read the technical content knowing the
author is selling something.

That bias does not make it a bad source. It makes it a precise record of what a
late-Victorian water factory actually bought, did, and worried about.

## Structure

- **Preface**, printed pp. iii–ix (scan 7–13) — a trade history and a jeremiad
  about adulteration
- **General Instructions**, pp. 11–21 (scan 15–25) — aeration, bottling,
  bottles and corks, factory, machinery, then ingredients: **water**, sugar,
  saccharin, carbonic acid gas, chemicals, flavouring agents
- **Fermentation**, pp. 21–25 — prevention, and the objectionable
  fermentations: acetous, mucoid, vinous
- **Sediments**, p. 26 · **Cleanliness** and **Filtration**, p. 27
- **Recipes**, pp. 28 onward — plain syrup, directions for mixing, then the
  flavoured aerated waters
- **Beer carbonating and bottling process** (scan ~2013 in extracted text)
- **Mineral Waters**, pp. 87–90 (scan 91–94) — the named-spring imitations
- **Non-exciseable cordials**, pp. 91 onward
- **Analyses — scale of charges**, p. 103 (scan 107)
- **Index**, and closing advertisements

Printed page + 4 = scan page, through the body. (Printed 14 / scan 18; printed

## What it is good evidence for

- [[water-quality-analysis]] — Bush's published fee schedule is a rare price on
  what a commercial water analysis cost in 1897
- [[filtration]] — charcoal filtration as routine factory practice, and the
  warning that a neglected filter is worse than none
- [[water-hardness-and-softening]] — hardness as a *product defect*, diagnosed
  through the deposit it leaves in the bottle
- [[artificial-carbonation]] — generator chemistry, bottling pressures,
  compressed CO₂ arriving as a commercial option
- [[mineral-waters]] — the named-spring imitation trade at industrial scale
- [[waterborne-contamination]] — lead pipes, lead in acids, mucoid fermentation
- [[preservatives-and-antiseptics]]

## Cautions

- OCR mangles the *æ* ligature throughout: *ærated* appears as `erated`,
  `srated`, `#rated`, `Alrated`. Normalise silently.
- Quantities are imperial and period-specific: Twaddell degrees for syrup
  gravity, cwt, drachms, grains per 10 oz bottle.
- Every "we recommend" is a sales pitch. Bush's Preservative and Bush's Soluble
  Essences are the house products.
