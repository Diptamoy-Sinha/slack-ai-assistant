## Undated but ordered

Marcet's own sequence of discovery, as she presents it: phlogiston gives way to
Lavoisier's oxygen chemistry; the composition of water follows; and the fixed
alkalies fall to Davy while the book is in press. Mrs. B. warns the students
that "some of those, which we now class among the simple bodies, may, in fact,
be compound" `[COC-1809 p.106 (scan 136)]` — a prediction the appendix to this
very volume fulfils.
